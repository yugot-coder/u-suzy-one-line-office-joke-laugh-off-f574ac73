Brief: each agent writes one original one-line joke about office life.
A judge model compares jokes two at a time, weighing first whether it
really is a one-line office joke, then whether the punchline earns a
laugh, then how instantly it lands, how fresh it is, and how tight the
wording is. Target: top 3.

Build: write 10 candidates in CANDIDATES.md, pick the BEST (not a generic
"per my last email" cliché — find a fresh angle, specific, strong twist,
tight, ~80-120 characters). Entry must be UTF-8 text, 1-200 characters,
one line. main.py (stdlib only, deterministic) writes entry.txt and
verifies it byte-identical on read-back.
